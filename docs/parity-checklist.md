# Meta Muse Parity Checklist

## Tasks, Email & Calendar

- [x] Automated Web Tasks & Bookings: fills multi-step web forms for travel, tables, appointments
- [ ] Background Task Resolution: finishes tasks after you close the app, pings you for decisions
- [ ] Email Delegation: reads, sorts, summarizes, drafts, and sends email for you
- [ ] Meeting & Calendar Coordination: resolves conflicts across calendars, protects focus time

## Research, Writing & Learning

- [x] Multi-Source Investigations: researches across the web and outputs summaries or tables
- [ ] Custom Audio Briefings: generates audio roundups and syncs them to Spotify
- [ ] Document & Content Drafting: writes reports, social posts, images, and podcasts
- [ ] Approachable Walkthroughs: turns a complex topic into a step-by-step guide with figures, in plain words

## People & Business Workflows

- [ ] Inner-Circle Profiling: stores contacts' preferences, locations, and milestones
- [ ] Relationship Nudges: suggests when to catch up, gift ideas, birthday greetings
- [ ] App Automation: triggers workflows in Notion, GitHub, Slack, Google Workspace, Teams
- [ ] Small Business Management: summarizes sales, drafts campaigns, handles invoices

## Interactive Apps, Artifacts & Games

- [x] Live Interactive Apps & Dashboards: builds working mini web apps in chat, like trackers and calculators
- [ ] Interactive Data Artifacts: builds live spreadsheets, trackers, and dashboards in chat
- [ ] Dynamic Spreadsheets & Tables: turns raw data into filterable, sortable, editable tables
- [ ] Embedded Approval Cards: shows UI widgets to review and authorize payments or email sends
- [ ] Shared Workspaces & Versioned Canvas: hosts artifacts at shareable URLs with collaboration and version history
- [ ] Interactive Timelines & Project Roadmaps: renders Gantt charts, milestones, and checklists
- [ ] Daily Puzzle Game: builds a new puzzle each day, keeps the streak and the history

## Design, Documents & Publishing

- [ ] Multi-Modal Visual Assets: generates images, mockups, social graphics, and marketing creatives
- [ ] Infographics & Data Visualizations: turns research and sales data into charts and flow diagrams
- [ ] UI/UX Wireframes & Specifications: produces wireframes, user flows, and PRDs as components or Markdown
- [ ] Built-In Design Tool for Phones: a standard design canvas like Claude Design, made for a phone screen: describe a design in chat, get screens and clickable prototypes, adjust them by touch
- [ ] Formatted Document Deliverables: exports slide outlines, PDF briefs, pitches, and proposals
- [ ] Code & Component Files: writes HTML/CSS/React files that run in a cloud sandbox or export to repos
- [ ] F-Droid Repo Hosting & Maintenance: hosts a personal F-Droid repo and keeps it current: builds, signs, syncs and publishes each app update
